Klipa Player is a player for the user's own M3U, M3U8 and Xtream-compatible sources. It ships no channels or other content and needs no account or sign-in.

To test playback:
1. Open the app and select "Add playlist URL".
2. Enter this playlist address and select Import: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KlipaTV/klipa-desktop/main/packaging/windows/store/certification-test.m3u
   It lists four open test streams (Big Buck Bunny from Mux, Tears of Steel from Unified Streaming and Apple's HLS reference streams). The public sample playlist https://klipa.tv/tools/api/sample-m3u works too.
3. Select a channel to play it. F or F11 toggles full screen, Esc leaves it, and Up/Down or Page Up/Page Down change channel.

"Support Klipa" opens https://klipa.tv/support/ in the browser: optional donations through GitHub Sponsors, a secure third-party payment (Store Policy 10.8.2). Paying unlocks or changes nothing in the app, and the app never asks for it.

The app makes no network request when it starts and installs no background service; it only contacts the addresses the user adds. The interface is in English.

Source code: https://github.com/KlipaTV/klipa-desktop
